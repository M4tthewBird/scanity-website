Frequently asked questions

What people ask most before they order their first scan.

What is a 3D scan from Scanity?

A photorealistic 3D copy of a real space or object, from a piece of jewellery to a whole building. You move and look around freely in the browser: no jumping between fixed points and no 360° photos.

How does the scanning work?

We come to the site and scan the space. You need no equipment of your own. We then process the footage into an accurate, photorealistic 3D model.

What do I get?

A link to the 3D viewer that you can share. You can also put the model on your own website: as a window (iframe), or right in the page so that it looks like part of it. From the same scan we can also make photos, a floor plan and a video walkthrough.

Do visitors need an app?

No. The 3D model opens in an ordinary browser on a computer, tablet or phone.

How much does it cost?

The price depends on the size and scope of the job. Tell us what you need scanned and we will send you a quote with no obligation.

How long does it take?

It depends on the scope of the job. We agree the dates of the scan and of the delivery in advance.

Where do you work?

In the Czech Republic, in Slovakia and, by arrangement, abroad.

What do you scan?

Homes for sale (real estate), hotels, sports venues and event spaces, construction sites and buildings and products for online stores.

Who is behind Scanity?

Scanity was founded by Matyáš Ptáček and Jaroslav Svoboda.

Do you have an object or space you'd like us to turn into 3D?

Tell us what you'd like to turn into 3D, and we'll find the best solution together.
