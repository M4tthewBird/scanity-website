Privacy Policy

Effective from 12 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data we process when you visit scanity.cz, view the 3D demos on it or write to us through the contact form, why we do so, and what rights you have. We follow Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Czech Act No. 110/2019 Coll. on the Processing of Personal Data. The Czech version of this policy prevails.

1. Who processes your data

The controller of your personal data is Matyáš Ptáček, a private individual (“Scanity” or “we”).

For anything about your personal data, contact us at matyas.ptacek@scanity.cz. We have not appointed a data protection officer, as the law does not require us to.

2. What data we process and why

3. How long we keep data

  • Enquiries and correspondence that do not lead to a project: at most 3 years from the last message, so we can pick up where we left off; then we delete them.
  • Data for a project: for the duration of the contract and then as long as the law requires (accounting records 10 years) or as needed to establish and defend legal claims (usually 3 years after the contract ends).
  • Server logs are kept only briefly by our hosting providers, under their own rules.

4. Who else handles the data

We process the data ourselves. To run the website and email we use the following providers, who process data for us as processors or receive it while delivering content:

  • EmailJS (emailjs.com) delivers messages from the contact form to our inbox.
  • Forpsi (INTERNET CZ, a.s.) runs our email inbox.
  • Netlify (Netlify, Inc., USA) hosts the website and the 3D viewer.
  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA) stores and delivers the 3D model files, downloaded only after you click “Load 3D viewer”.
  • Google Fonts (Google Ireland Limited) delivers the Manrope typeface.
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) delivers the EmailJS library, only once you scroll to the contact form.

Loading a page necessarily sends your IP address to these providers' servers; they could not deliver the content otherwise. Beyond that, we share data only where the law requires it, for example at the request of a public authority.

5. Transfers outside the European Union

Some of these providers are based in, or have servers outside, the EU, in particular in the USA. Such transfers take place only with safeguards under Chapter V GDPR: the European Commission's adequacy decision (the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) where the provider is bound by it, or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

6. Cookies and browser storage

The website uses no cookies and no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. It keeps only a few technical preferences in your browser's storage (localStorage). They are not personal data and are not sent anywhere:

  • that you have already seen the hint on how to control the 3D demo (on the website and in the viewer),
  • settings you chose in the 3D viewer, such as performance mode, controls or showing labels.

You can clear these preferences at any time in your browser settings.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your data and receive a copy (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • have your data erased where we no longer need it or may not process it (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • restrict processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • data portability for data you gave us (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR).

To exercise your rights, email matyas.ptacek@scanity.cz. We will reply without undue delay and within one month at the latest. If you believe we handle your data unlawfully, you can lodge a complaint with the Czech data protection authority, the Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů), Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Prague 7, uoou.gov.cz, or with the authority in your EU country of residence.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add a new service. The current version is always on this page, with its effective date at the top.
